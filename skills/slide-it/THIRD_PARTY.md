# Third-party credits

slide-it is written in its own words. Where an idea or value came from someone else's work, it is credited here.

- **Emil Kowalski, [emilkowalski/skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills)** (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Emil Kowalski): the motion durations and easing behind the `--dur` and `--ease-out` tokens (`kit/TOKENS.md`).
- **[Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable)** by Paul Bakaus (Apache-2.0): ideas behind each style's "Steer clear of" line and the one-pass check (SKILL.md, step 6), rewritten in our words. No Impeccable text is copied.
- **[transitions.dev](https://transitions.dev/)**: ideas only (state attributes such as `data-state`, short staggers). No code or text copied.
- **[devigner UI](https://ui.devigner.cc/components)**: the idea of a compact list that widens into a panel, used for the questions menu in `kit/screens.html`, re-implemented from scratch. No code copied.
- **[Anatomy](https://github.com/wheresryan22/anatomy)** by Ryan (MIT): the isometric drawing kit in `kit/figures/` is a browser build of Anatomy's `kit/iso-kit.ts`, `iso.css` and the smooth, burst and settle helpers from `kit/gl.mjs` (colours re-mapped to our tokens); `figures.md` adapts its craft rules in our own words. Licence below.
- **[Hairline](https://github.com/lucasmarkes/hairline)** by Lucas Marques (MIT): the idea of a flat drawing in one stroke weight (`figures.md`); no Hairline code is copied.

The example pages cite their own sources in their Full notes.
