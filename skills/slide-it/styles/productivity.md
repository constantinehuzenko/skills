# Personal productivity

Looks like Things 3, Notion, Todoist. For to-do lists, habit trackers, weekly planning, goals, time budgets, reading lists, routines and decision journals.

**Colour:** Restrained warm paper with one marigold-orange accent; teal is "done and focus"; the pop is project colours (teal, violet, pink dots) and a highlighter-yellow `--tint` on today.

**Light** `--bg:#faf8f4;--surface:#fff;--fg:#1f2328;--muted:#5d6168;--line:#e6e2d8;--accent:#a84a06;--accent-fg:#fff;--good:#1b7f3a;--warn:#7a4d00;--bad:#c2301f;--accent-2:#0b7285;--tint:#fff2b3;`

**Dark** `--bg:#141311;--surface:#1d1b18;--fg:#f2efe9;--muted:#aaa49a;--line:#34312b;--accent:#ffb04d;--accent-fg:#231300;--good:#5fd68f;--warn:#e8c15a;--bad:#ff7b6b;--accent-2:#4cc9d9;--tint:#3d3510;`

**Type:** Albert Sans 600/700 display and 400/500 body, tabular numerals for times. `Albert+Sans:wght@400;500;600;700`. Fallback: sans `system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif`.

**Shape:** radius 8 to 10, 1px lines, compact 44 to 48px rows, list-first.


**Motion:** small and satisfying: the check draws, rows reorder with FLIP, quick.

Steer clear of: grey-on-grey, confetti and streak guilt, five-level nested lists, a full-width calendar on a phone.
