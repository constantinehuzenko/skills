# Kids & family

Looks like PBS Kids, LEGO, Khan Academy Kids. For kids' activities, chore charts, learning games, family calendars and meal plans, kids' birthdays and school routines (adult family logistics: home.md's tokens).

**Colour:** Full palette at high chroma: toy blue, sun yellow, plus pink and green helpers, on a pastel sky ground.

**Light** `--bg:#f1f8ff;--surface:#fff;--fg:#172554;--muted:#4a5d8a;--line:#d6e6fa;--accent:#1461c4;--accent-fg:#fff;--good:#1b7f3a;--warn:#8f5200;--bad:#d32f2f;--accent-2:#f7b500;--tint:#ffe9a8;`

**Dark** `--bg:#0f1a3a;--surface:#182654;--fg:#f0f5ff;--muted:#a9b9e0;--line:#2b3c70;--accent:#7db4ff;--accent-fg:#051a3d;--good:#5fdc8a;--warn:#ffc233;--bad:#ff7b7b;--accent-2:#ffd23f;--tint:#243a78;`

**Type:** Fredoka 600/700 display + Nunito 400/700 body, 18px up. `Fredoka:wght@500;600;700` `Nunito:wght@400;700`. Fallback: sans `system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif`.

**Shape:** radius 22 to 28, pill buttons at least 56px tall, 3px lines, low density, huge hit areas.


**Motion:** the one genre where a tapped sticker may spring slightly (short); reduced motion removes it.

Steer clear of: Comic Sans, clutter, tiny targets, ad-like pop-ups, red-only errors, babyish for older kids.

--accent-2 is a fill only, never text.
