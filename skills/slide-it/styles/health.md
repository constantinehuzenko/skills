# Health, body & science

Looks like Apple Health, Oura, Healthline. For sleep, nutrition, training load, conditions and symptoms, anatomy, study summaries, supplements, lab results and science explainers (physics or lab-heavy pages: developer.md's tokens with labelled linework).

**Colour:** Restrained mint-white ground, Committed teal-green in rings, trends and the selected state; coral is the heart-rate and alert voice.

**Light** `--bg:#f2f8f5;--surface:#fff;--fg:#12261f;--muted:#4a6258;--line:#d8e8e0;--accent:#0a7565;--accent-fg:#fff;--good:#2b7d2e;--warn:#8a5a00;--bad:#c0392b;--accent-2:#d9534f;--tint:#dff3ec;`

**Dark** `--bg:#0b1a16;--surface:#12261f;--fg:#e6f4ee;--muted:#96b5a8;--line:#1f3a31;--accent:#4fd1b5;--accent-fg:#04201a;--good:#8fdc7a;--warn:#f2b84d;--bad:#ff8a7a;--accent-2:#ff9a8c;--tint:#0f3a30;`

**Type:** Lexend 500/700 display + Source Sans 3 body. `Lexend:wght@500;700` `Source+Sans+3:wght@400;600`. Fallback: sans `system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif`.

**Shape:** radius 18, soft, low density, big calm numbers, plenty of space.


**Motion:** slow and breathing, rings fill once.

Steer clear of: scare-red, stethoscope and heart-cross clip-art, miracle-claim styling, clinical walls of text.
