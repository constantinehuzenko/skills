# History & stories

Looks like Smithsonian Magazine, The Guardian Long Read, Atlas Obscura. For timelines, biographies, how something happened, places and eras, archive-based narratives and long reads.

**Colour:** Restrained sepia: parchment ground, umber accent, ink blue and ochre in maps and era bands.

**Light** `--bg:#f4ead8;--surface:#fbf5e8;--fg:#2a2018;--muted:#6a5946;--line:#dccdb2;--accent:#8a3a16;--accent-fg:#fff;--good:#3d6b45;--warn:#8a5800;--bad:#a8281c;--accent-2:#2c4a7c;--tint:#ead9bc;`

**Dark** `--bg:#1c150e;--surface:#2a2016;--fg:#f3e8d3;--muted:#c4b095;--line:#473726;--accent:#e8a072;--accent-fg:#2a1204;--good:#93c9a0;--warn:#e8b45a;--bad:#ff8a78;--accent-2:#8fb0e8;--tint:#33261a;`

**Type:** Libre Caslon Text 400/700 display (italic for quotes) + Work Sans body. `Libre+Caslon+Text:ital,wght@0,400;0,700;1,400` `Work+Sans:wght@400;500;600`. Fallback: serif `Georgia,"Times New Roman",serif` for display, sans `system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif` for body.

**Shape:** radius 2 to 4, hairline rules, moderate air.


**Motion:** slow dissolves, scrub-driven.

Steer clear of: a sepia filter over everything, tea-stained fake paper, scroll and parchment clip-art, ye-olde fonts.
