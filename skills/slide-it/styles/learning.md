# Learning & explainers for a general audience

Looks like Khan Academy, Duolingo, Brilliant. For how-it-works pages, concept explainers, study guides, quizzes, languages, and math and money literacy.

**Colour:** Full palette: grape primary, sun-yellow fills, green for "got it". Bright and friendly, not childish.

**Light** `--bg:#faf7ff;--surface:#fff;--fg:#1d1133;--muted:#5b4d78;--line:#e5dcf5;--accent:#8a2fc4;--accent-fg:#fff;--good:#1a7a45;--warn:#8f5200;--bad:#c2301f;--accent-2:#f5b400;--tint:#f0e3fb;`

**Dark** `--bg:#150d24;--surface:#20153a;--fg:#f3ecff;--muted:#b7a8d6;--line:#35265a;--accent:#d59bff;--accent-fg:#1d0833;--good:#5fd68f;--warn:#f5b94a;--bad:#ff7a7a;--accent-2:#ffc933;--tint:#2e1b52;`

**Type:** Rubik 600/700 display + Atkinson Hyperlegible body, 17px up. `Rubik:wght@500;600;700` `Atkinson+Hyperlegible:wght@400;700`. Fallback: sans `system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif`.

**Shape:** radius 16 to 24, 2px `--line` borders, chunky buttons (press = scale only), generous type.


**Motion:** quick and bright; a correct answer gets one small scale-up, a wrong one a tint and a sentence, no shake.

Steer clear of: textbook sidebars, Comic Sans, cartoon owls and lightbulbs, confetti on everything, talking down.

--accent-2 is a fill only, never text.
