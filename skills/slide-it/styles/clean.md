# Clean, with colour

Looks like a clean, modern app. For a one-off subject or a mixed bag, when no other style fits.

**Colour:** Teal with an orange second voice on a tinted ground.

**Light** `--bg:#f5faf9;--surface:#fff;--fg:#10262b;--muted:#4d666b;--line:#d7e7e6;--accent:#0a7280;--accent-fg:#fff;--good:#1b7f4b;--warn:#94570a;--bad:#c2301f;--accent-2:#e8691c;--tint:#dcf2f1;`

**Dark** `--bg:#0b1719;--surface:#112427;--fg:#e8f4f4;--muted:#94b3b6;--line:#1f3a3e;--accent:#4fd0dc;--accent-fg:#03181b;--good:#4fd69a;--warn:#f2b441;--bad:#ff7b6b;--accent-2:#ffa35c;--tint:#0f3a3f;`

**Type:** Onest 600/700 display + Onest 400/500 body. `Onest:wght@400;500;600;700`. Fallback: sans `system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif`.

**Shape:** radius 12, 1px lines, medium density.


**Motion:** quick and calm: values ease to their new state, screens slide in as they arrive.

Steer clear of: plain grey and a blue button.
