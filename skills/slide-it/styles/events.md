# Events & weddings

Looks like Zola, The Knot, Minted. For wedding plans, budgets, seating, RSVPs, showers, parties, reunions and run-of-show.

**Colour:** Soft Full palette at low chroma: ivory ground, champagne bronze as the accent, blush and sage as washes.

**Light** `--bg:#fbf7f1;--surface:#fffdfa;--fg:#3a2c2b;--muted:#75625f;--line:#eadfd2;--accent:#86601f;--accent-fg:#fff;--good:#3f7552;--warn:#8a5a14;--bad:#a8382f;--accent-2:#5f8a6e;--tint:#f7e3df;`

**Dark** `--bg:#1d1517;--surface:#2a1f21;--fg:#f7ece6;--muted:#c7b0a8;--line:#45343a;--accent:#e6bd7a;--accent-fg:#2a1a05;--good:#8fcfa5;--warn:#f0b960;--bad:#ff8d80;--accent-2:#9fc7ac;--tint:#36232a;`

**Type:** Cormorant Garamond 500/600 display at 28px and up only (italic for names) + Jost body. `Cormorant+Garamond:ital,wght@0,500;0,600;1,500` `Jost:wght@400;500`. Fallback: serif `Georgia,"Times New Roman",serif` for display, sans `system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif` for body.

**Shape:** radius 16 to 22, arch-topped image frames (full top radius), 1px lines. The one genre that earns plenty of air.


**Motion:** slow and soft (`--dur-slow`), one gentle reveal, no confetti by reflex.

Steer clear of: script fonts for body text, clip-art doves and rings, glitter or gold gradients, hot pink, grey pastels.
