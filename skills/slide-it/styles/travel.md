# Travel & trips

Looks like Airbnb, Google Travel, Wanderlog. For itineraries, destination guides, trip budgets, packing, routes, flight and hotel comparisons, road trips and where-to-go decisions.

**Colour:** Full palette: ocean blue and sunset coral on warm sand, sky wash for headers. Sunny, never corporate.

**Light** `--bg:#fbf6ee;--surface:#fff;--fg:#12303d;--muted:#50686f;--line:#e8dfd0;--accent:#0a6fb0;--accent-fg:#fff;--good:#11784f;--warn:#99560a;--bad:#c4361f;--accent-2:#e0502f;--tint:#dff0fa;`

**Dark** `--bg:#0b1e29;--surface:#12303d;--fg:#eaf4f7;--muted:#9dbac4;--line:#214656;--accent:#6bbcf2;--accent-fg:#04202c;--good:#4fd69a;--warn:#f5b94a;--bad:#ff8070;--accent-2:#ff8f70;--tint:#0f3a52;`

**Type:** Plus Jakarta Sans 700/800 display + DM Sans body. `Plus+Jakarta+Sans:wght@600;700;800` `DM+Sans:wght@400;500;700`. Fallback: sans `system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif`.

**Shape:** radius 16 to 20, pill chips, soft lines, photo-led, roomy.


**Motion:** the route line draws itself, pins settle in, day tabs slide.

Steer clear of: stock postcards, globe-and-plane clip-art, passport-stamp costume, dotted world-map wallpaper, everything blue.
