# Data, analytics & finance

Looks like Mercury, Wealthfront, Stripe Dashboard. For market sizing, budgets, ROI, pricing, loans, retirement, KPIs, dashboards and cost-benefit, or a spreadsheet turned into a page (illustration, not advice).

**Colour:** Restrained ice-blue ground; Committed ultramarine fills about a third of every chart; mint is the comparison series; deltas use `--good/--bad` with arrows drawn in SVG.

**Light** `--bg:#f1f5fb;--surface:#fff;--fg:#0c1b33;--muted:#475a78;--line:#d9e2f0;--accent:#1d4fd7;--accent-fg:#fff;--good:#0c7a43;--warn:#8f5500;--bad:#c2301f;--accent-2:#0a8f7f;--tint:#e3ecfd;`

**Dark** `--bg:#0a1222;--surface:#111c33;--fg:#e7edf8;--muted:#93a3c0;--line:#22304d;--accent:#7ea6ff;--accent-fg:#06122b;--good:#4fd69a;--warn:#f2b441;--bad:#ff7a6b;--accent-2:#3fd0bd;--tint:#14264d;`

**Type:** Manrope 600/800 display (800 for the big figure) + Public Sans body + IBM Plex Mono for table figures. Tabular numerals on every figure. `Manrope:wght@600;800` `Public+Sans:wght@400;600` `IBM+Plex+Mono:wght@400;500`. Fallback: sans `system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif`, mono `ui-monospace,"SF Mono",Menlo,Consolas,monospace`.

**Shape:** radius 12, 1px lines, medium density, figures right-aligned.


**Motion:** numbers roll, the chart draws once left to right, sliders tween.

Steer clear of: green/red as the only signal, 3D pies, glow under areas, handshake stock art, advice-sounding claims.
