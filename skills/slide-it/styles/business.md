# Business & strategy

Looks like Ramp, Pitch, Attio. For verdicts, go/no-go, strategy memos, market entry, org and hiring plans, roadmaps, competitor reviews and board updates (modern business tools, not newspapers).

**Colour:** Committed. A decision amber on warm white, near-black ink, teal as the counter-option. Confident and product-like, not corporate blue.

**Light** `--bg:#f8f7f3;--surface:#ffffff;--fg:#16150f;--muted:#5d5a50;--line:#e4e1d6;--accent:#b45309;--accent-fg:#fff;--good:#15803d;--warn:#a16207;--bad:#b91c1c;--accent-2:#f5b301;--tint:#fdf3d8;`

**Dark** `--bg:#13120e;--surface:#1c1b16;--fg:#f3f1ea;--muted:#a9a596;--line:#2f2d25;--accent:#f5b301;--accent-fg:#1a1300;--good:#4ade80;--warn:#facc15;--bad:#f87171;--accent-2:#2dd4bf;--tint:#2a2410;`

**Type:** Schibsted Grotesk 700/800 display (big, tight, the verdict reads like a product headline) + Hanken Grotesk body + JetBrains Mono for small labels and figures. `Schibsted+Grotesk:wght@700;800` `Hanken+Grotesk:wght@400;500;600` `JetBrains+Mono:wght@400;500`. Fallback: sans `system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif`, mono `ui-monospace,"SF Mono",Menlo,Consolas,monospace`.

**Shape:** radius 10 to 14, 1px lines, the main control in one raised panel, labels in small mono.


**Motion:** snappy; the verdict pill flips, bars slide to their new length.

Steer clear of: newspaper costume (salmon paper, headline serif), consultant clip-art (puzzle pieces, target arrows), SmartArt pyramids, a bullet wall as "executive summary", the blue-and-grey default.

--accent-2 is a fill only, never text.
