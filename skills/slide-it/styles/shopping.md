# Shopping & product comparison

Looks like Wirecutter, Apple Store compare, RTINGS. For buying guides, product vs product, picks, deals, spec tables, gear lists and "which one should I buy".

**Colour:** Restrained crisp white; Committed magenta on the pick and the buy button; gold is the "Best pick" award; the product art carries the rest.

**Light** `--bg:#fff;--surface:#f6f6f8;--fg:#14141a;--muted:#55556a;--line:#e3e3ea;--accent:#c0185f;--accent-fg:#fff;--good:#12784a;--warn:#8f5300;--bad:#c2301f;--accent-2:#b8860b;--tint:#fde6f0;`

**Dark** `--bg:#0e0e12;--surface:#1a1a22;--fg:#f4f4f8;--muted:#a6a6bb;--line:#2c2c38;--accent:#ff7ab3;--accent-fg:#2a0715;--good:#4fd69a;--warn:#f2b441;--bad:#ff7a6b;--accent-2:#f0c040;--tint:#3a1428;`

**Type:** Instrument Sans 600/700 for display and body. Tabular numerals on prices. `Instrument+Sans:wght@400;500;600;700`. Fallback: sans `system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif`.

**Shape:** radius 12, 1px lines, crisp product-card density.


**Motion:** snappy reorder, hover lift behind `(hover: hover)`.

Steer clear of: affiliate-site clutter, star-rating spam, fake discounts or urgency timers, banner-ad styling.

--accent-2 is a fill only, never text.
