# Home, plants & lifestyle

Looks like The Sill, IKEA, Apartment Therapy. For home projects, room layouts, plant care, gardening calendars, decor, organising, routines and hosting.

**Type:** Fraunces 600 display (soft serif) + Hanken Grotesk body. `Fraunces:opsz,wght@9..144,500;9..144,600` `Hanken+Grotesk:wght@400;500;600`. Fallback: serif `Georgia,"Times New Roman",serif` for display, sans `system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif` for body.
