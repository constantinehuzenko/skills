# Sports & fitness

Looks like Nike Training Club, Strava, ESPN. For training plans, race pacing, workout logs, league and team stats, matchups, gym programmes and recovery.

**Colour:** Drenched dark (near-black with volt lime) is the home theme; light is white with deep lime and an orange effort colour. Design dark first.

**Light** `--bg:#f4f5f1;--surface:#fff;--fg:#0f1410;--muted:#4d554a;--line:#dde1d8;--accent:#4a7a0a;--accent-fg:#fff;--good:#1b7f3a;--warn:#94590a;--bad:#c62828;--accent-2:#e8500f;--tint:#e7f6c9;`

**Dark** `--bg:#0a0c09;--surface:#151a12;--fg:#f1f5ea;--muted:#a1ab96;--line:#263020;--accent:#c6ff3d;--accent-fg:#0c1400;--good:#5ee08a;--warn:#ffb23a;--bad:#ff6b5e;--accent-2:#ff7a33;--tint:#1f2a10;`

**Type:** Barlow Condensed 700/800 uppercase display + Barlow body. `Barlow+Condensed:wght@600;700;800` `Barlow:wght@400;500;600`. Fallback: condensed `"Arial Narrow","Helvetica Neue",Arial,sans-serif` for display, sans `system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif` for body.

**Shape:** radius 6 (or 0 with one cut corner), 2px lines, dense, huge numerals.


**Motion:** fast and snappy, numbers count up with a strong ease-out.

Steer clear of: neon glow, athlete silhouettes, flame and lightning clip-art, motivational slogans.
